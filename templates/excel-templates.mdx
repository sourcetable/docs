Sourcetable maintains a library of over 1,000 Excel-compatible templates that you can import and use immediately. These templates come with formulas, formatting, and sample data — ready to populate with your own information.

## Using a template

1. Browse the template library at [sourcetable.com/excel-templates](https://sourcetable.com/excel-templates)
2. Select a template that fits your use case
3. Click to open it in Sourcetable
4. Replace sample data with your own
5. Use the AI assistant to customize or extend it
