Sourcetable offers two ways to work with templates: choose from a library of pre-built templates, or describe what you need and let the AI generate one from scratch.

## AI template generator

Open the AI chat and select **Template** mode, or just ask:

- "Create a monthly budget tracker template"
- "Generate a project timeline template with milestones and dependencies"
- "Make a sales pipeline template with stages, probabilities, and expected close dates"
- "Build an employee onboarding checklist template"

The AI creates a fully structured spreadsheet with:
- Appropriate columns and headers
- Formulas for calculations (totals, averages, percentages)
- Conditional formatting for visual cues
- Sample data to demonstrate the template

## Customizing templates

After the AI generates a template, you can:

- Modify columns, headers, and formulas
- Adjust formatting and colors
- Ask the AI to add or remove sections
- Connect it to live data sources via connectors

## Saving templates

Save any workbook as a template for reuse:

1. Build your template (manually or with AI)
2. Save the workbook
3. Duplicate it each time you need a fresh copy

Templates connected to data sources will pull fresh data each time they're opened.
