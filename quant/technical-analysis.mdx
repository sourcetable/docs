Sourcetable's technical analysis tools provide indicator-based analysis, options pricing models, and futures analysis.

## Technical analyst

The technical analyst calculates and interprets common indicators:

- **Moving averages** — SMA, EMA, WMA
- **Momentum** — RSI, MACD, Stochastic Oscillator
- **Volatility** — Bollinger Bands, ATR, historical volatility
- **Volume** — OBV, volume-weighted average price (VWAP)
- **Trend** — ADX, Parabolic SAR, Ichimoku Cloud

## Options pricing

The futures analyst includes options pricing models:

| Model | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Black-Scholes** | European option pricing with constant volatility |
| **Heston** | Stochastic volatility model for more realistic pricing |
| **SABR** | Stochastic alpha beta rho for interest rate derivatives |

### Greeks
Calculate the key option sensitivity measures:
- **Delta** — price sensitivity to underlying
- **Gamma** — rate of change of delta
- **Theta** — time decay
- **Vega** — sensitivity to volatility
- **Rho** — sensitivity to interest rates

## Using technical analysis

Ask the AI:

- "Run a technical analysis on TSLA with RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands"
- "Show me the 50-day and 200-day moving averages for SPY"
- "Price a call option on AAPL with strike $200, expiry in 30 days"
- "Calculate the Greeks for my options portfolio"
- "Is NVDA showing a golden cross or death cross signal?"

## Chart generation

Technical analysis results can be visualized as candlestick charts with overlaid indicators. Ask the AI to generate charts alongside the analysis for a visual representation of the signals.
