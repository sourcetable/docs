Sourcetable integrates with Robinhood to let you execute trades directly from your spreadsheet. Analyze a stock, make a decision, and place the trade — all in one place.

<Warning>
  Trading involves risk of loss. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.
</Warning>

## Supported assets

- **Stocks** — buy and sell equities
- **Crypto** — trade cryptocurrencies
- **Fractional shares** — buy partial shares of expensive stocks

## Paper trading

Test your strategies without risking real money using paper trading mode. Paper trades simulate real market conditions but don't execute actual transactions.

## Placing a trade

Ask the AI:

- "Buy 10 shares of AAPL at market price"
- "Sell 5 shares of TSLA"
- "Buy $500 worth of Bitcoin"
- "Place a limit order for 100 shares of MSFT at $400"

## Connecting Robinhood

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add credentials">
    In the AI chat, provide your Robinhood credentials when prompted. These are encrypted and stored securely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Complete any required two-factor authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start trading">
    Ask the AI to execute trades or check your positions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Research tools

The Robinhood integration also provides research capabilities:

- View current positions and portfolio value
- Check real-time stock prices and quotes
- Review order history
- Analyze portfolio performance

## Workflow example

A typical analysis-to-execution workflow:

1. Run fundamental analysis on a stock
2. Check technical indicators for entry timing
3. Assess portfolio risk impact of adding the position
4. Execute the trade through Robinhood
5. Monitor the position with automated reporting
