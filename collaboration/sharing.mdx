Sourcetable provides several ways to share your work with others.

## Sharing workbooks

Share workbooks with team members in your organization. Members can view and collaborate on shared workbooks within the team workspace.

## Publishing charts

Publish charts to create shareable links:

1. Create a chart in your workbook
2. Click **Publish** on the chart
3. Share the generated link with anyone

Published charts display the latest data, so recipients always see current information.

## Exporting for sharing

Export your work in multiple formats for stakeholders who don't use Sourcetable:

- **PDF** — formatted reports and dashboards
- **XLSX** — Excel-compatible workbooks
- **CSV** — raw data for other tools
- **Doc** — document format for reports

## Referrals

Invite colleagues to Sourcetable through the referral program. Access referral links from your account settings.

## Enterprise sharing

Enterprise plans include additional sharing and security features:

- **SSO (Single Sign-On)** — centralized authentication
- **SOC 2 Type 2** compliance
- **HIPAA** compliance
- **PCI** compliance
- **Encrypted at rest** — all data encrypted
- **Private user keys** — additional encryption layer
- **Cloud or on-premises** — deployment flexibility
