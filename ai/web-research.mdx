title: Web research
description: Search the web, find data, and import information directly into your spreadsheet with AI.
---

Sourcetable's AI can browse the internet, search for information, and pull data directly into your spreadsheet.

## Web search

Ask the AI to search the web for information:

- "Search for the current S&P 500 companies and their market caps"
- "Find the latest GDP data for G7 countries"
- "Look up competitor pricing for project management tools"

The AI uses web search and Google search tools to find relevant information and formats it into spreadsheet-ready data.

## Deep research

For complex research tasks that require synthesizing multiple sources, use the deep research capability:

- "Research the electric vehicle market — key players, market size, growth rate, and trends"
- "Compile a competitive analysis of CRM platforms with features, pricing, and market share"

The AI searches multiple sources, cross-references information, and produces a structured summary.

## Public datasets

Select **Public Dataset** mode to find and import publicly available datasets:

- "Find a dataset of US census data by zip code"
- "Get historical stock prices for AAPL for the last 5 years"
- "Find a dataset of global COVID-19 case counts"

The AI uses the data finder tool to locate relevant public datasets and imports them into your spreadsheet.

## Browser automation

For more advanced data collection, the AI can automate browser interactions using Playwright:

- Navigate to specific web pages
- Extract structured data from websites
- Take screenshots of web content
- Interact with web applications

<Warning>
  Browser automation features may be subject to website terms of service. Use responsibly and respect robots.txt directives.
</Warning>
