The AI chat is the primary way to interact with Sourcetable's AI. Open it with `Cmd+\` (Mac) or `Ctrl+\` (Windows) and ask questions about your data in plain English.

## How it works

1. The AI reads the data in your spreadsheet (based on your scope settings)
2. You ask a question or give an instruction in natural language
3. The AI responds with numbers, text, formulas, charts, tables, or a combination — whatever fits the question
4. You can follow up with additional questions in the same conversation thread

## Example prompts

**Analysis:**
- "What are the top 5 customers by revenue?"
- "Is there a correlation between marketing spend and new signups?"
- "What's the month-over-month growth rate?"

**Manipulation:**
- "Sort this data by date descending"
- "Remove all rows where status is 'Cancelled'"
- "Add a column that calculates profit margin as (Revenue - Cost) / Revenue"

**Summarization:**
- "Summarize the key trends in this dataset"
- "Give me a one-paragraph executive summary of Q4 performance"

**Generation:**
- "Generate 100 rows of sample e-commerce order data"
- "Create a template for a monthly budget tracker"

## Thread history

Your conversations are saved as threads. Access previous threads from the sidebar in the chat panel. Each thread maintains its context, so you can pick up where you left off.

## Inserting AI responses

When the AI generates data, formulas, or tables, you can insert them directly into your spreadsheet:

- Click the **Insert** button on any AI response to write it into your cells
- Use the **Copy** button to copy the response to your clipboard
- Use the **Download** button to export responses as files

## Attaching files

Click the file upload button in the chat to attach CSV, XLSX, JSON, or other files. The AI can analyze these files directly without you needing to import them into the spreadsheet first.

## Selecting a model

Pro and Max plan users can choose which AI model powers their chat. Click the model selector dropdown to switch between available models. See [AI models](/reference/ai-models) for the full list.
