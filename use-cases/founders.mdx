Sourcetable gives founders a single source of truth for the metrics that matter — MRR, burn, pipeline, and headcount — updated live from your actual data.

## What founders do in Sourcetable

- **MRR tracking** — connect Stripe and see revenue metrics update in real time
- **Burn rate** — track spending against runway with live accounting data
- **Pipeline visibility** — connect your CRM and see deal flow without logging into Salesforce
- **Investor reporting** — generate board deck metrics and investor updates in minutes

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| MRR dashboard | Connect Stripe, ask "Show MRR, churn rate, and ARPU for the last 12 months" |
| Runway calculator | Connect QuickBooks, ask "At current burn rate, how many months of runway do we have?" |
| Board deck metrics | Ask "Generate a summary of all key metrics for our monthly investor update" |
| Hiring plan | Ask "Model headcount costs at 3, 5, and 8 new hires per quarter" |
