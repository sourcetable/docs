## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|------|-------------|
| KPI dashboard | Connect Stripe and your database, then ask "Build a dashboard with MRR, churn, CAC, and LTV" |
| Financial projections | "Create a 12-month revenue forecast based on current growth rate and seasonality" |
| Investor reporting | "Generate a monthly investor update with key metrics, trends, and commentary" |
| Competitive analysis | Use deep research to "Compare our pricing, features, and positioning to [competitors]" |
| Unit economics | "Calculate CAC, LTV, and payback period by acquisition channel" |
| Fundraising prep | "Analyze our metrics against Series A benchmarks for SaaS companies" |
