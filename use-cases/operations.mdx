Sourcetable helps ops teams build the reports nobody wants to build manually — SLA tracking, capacity planning, and business reviews from live data.

## What operations teams do in Sourcetable

- **SLA tracking** — monitor service level agreements across tools and flag breaches
- **Capacity planning** — forecast demand and resource needs
- **Business reviews** — auto-generate weekly and monthly operational summaries
- **Process optimization** — identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies in your data

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| SLA monitoring | Connect Zendesk, ask "Show SLA compliance rates by team and flag breaches" |
| Capacity forecast | Upload demand data, ask "Forecast capacity needs for the next quarter" |
| Weekly ops report | Connect data sources, ask "Generate a weekly business review" |
| Bottleneck analysis | Ask "Where are the longest cycle times in our workflow?" |
