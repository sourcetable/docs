## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|------|-------------|
| SLA monitoring | Connect your ticketing system and ask "Show SLA compliance by team and priority" |
| Capacity planning | "Forecast support ticket volume for the next month and calculate staffing needs" |
| Process optimization | "Identify bottlenecks in our order fulfillment process from the operations data" |
| Vendor analysis | "Compare vendor performance by cost, delivery time, and quality metrics" |
| Business reviews | "Create a monthly operations dashboard with KPIs, trends, and anomalies" |
| Inventory tracking | "Flag products below reorder point and calculate optimal order quantities" |
