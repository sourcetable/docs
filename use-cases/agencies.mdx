Sourcetable helps agencies produce client reports, campaign analytics, and performance dashboards at scale — without rebuilding reports from scratch each month.

## What agencies do in Sourcetable

- **Client reporting** — connect each client's ad accounts and analytics, generate branded reports
- **Campaign analytics** — cross-platform performance analysis across Google, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn
- **Performance dashboards** — live dashboards that clients can access
- **Competitive research** — use AI to research competitors and market trends for clients

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Monthly client report | Connect Google Ads + GA4, ask "Generate a monthly performance report with ROAS, CPA, and conversion trends" |
| Cross-platform analytics | Connect all ad platforms, ask "Compare performance across Google, Facebook, and TikTok" |
| SEO audit | Connect Google Search Console, ask "Show keyword rankings, impressions, and click-through rates" |
| Competitive analysis | Ask "Research the top 5 competitors in [industry] and summarize their positioning" |
