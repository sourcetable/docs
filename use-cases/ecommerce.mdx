Sourcetable connects to your e-commerce stack and gives you instant analytics on revenue, inventory, and customers — without waiting for BI team bandwidth.

## What e-commerce teams do in Sourcetable

- **Revenue analytics** — track sales by product, channel, and time period
- **Inventory forecasting** — predict when stock runs out and optimize reorder timing
- **Customer LTV** — calculate and segment customers by lifetime value
- **Product performance** — identify best sellers, underperformers, and trends

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Revenue dashboard | Connect Shopify + Stripe, ask "Show daily revenue by product category" |
| Inventory forecast | Ask "When will we run out of stock on our top 10 products at current sell-through rates?" |
| Customer cohort analysis | Ask "Show retention rates by signup cohort" |
| Ad ROI | Connect Shopify + Google Ads, ask "Which campaigns have the best ROAS?" |
