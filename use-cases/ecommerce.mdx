## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|------|-------------|
| Product performance | Connect Shopify and ask "Show me top products by revenue, margin, and growth rate" |
| Customer LTV | "Calculate customer lifetime value by acquisition channel and cohort" |
| Pricing optimization | "Analyze price elasticity for our top 50 SKUs" |
| Inventory forecasting | "Forecast demand for each product category for the next 90 days" |
| Basket analysis | "Which products are frequently bought together? Show association rules" |
| Ad ROAS | Connect ad platforms and ask "Show ROAS by channel, campaign, and product category" |
