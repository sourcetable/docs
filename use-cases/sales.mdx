Sourcetable connects to your CRM and gives you instant visibility into pipeline, rep performance, and forecast accuracy — without building reports from scratch.

## What sales teams do in Sourcetable

- **Pipeline analysis** — see deal velocity, stage conversion rates, and pipeline value at a glance
- **Rep scorecards** — track quota attainment, activity metrics, and win rates per rep
- **Forecast accuracy** — compare forecasts to actuals and identify patterns
- **Win/loss analysis** — understand what factors predict closed deals

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Pipeline dashboard | Connect Salesforce, ask "Show pipeline by stage with expected close dates" |
| Rep performance | Ask "Rank reps by quota attainment and show their win rates" |
| Deal prediction | Ask "Predict which open deals are most likely to close based on historical patterns" |
| Territory analysis | Ask "Break down revenue by territory and identify underperforming regions" |
