Connect your CRM to Sourcetable to analyze pipeline health, forecast revenue, and identify opportunities.

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|------|-------------|
| Pipeline analysis | Connect Salesforce and ask "Show me pipeline by stage, rep, and expected close date" |
| Revenue forecasting | "Forecast next quarter's revenue based on historical win rates and current pipeline" |
| Rep performance | "Rank sales reps by quota attainment and show their deal velocity trends" |
| Lead scoring | "Build a lead scoring model based on historical conversion data" |
| Territory analysis | "Compare revenue per territory and identify underperforming regions" |
| Churn prediction | "Which accounts are most likely to churn based on engagement metrics?" |
