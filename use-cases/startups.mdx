Sourcetable is the analytics team you don't have yet. Connect your data, ask questions, and get the same analysis a full-time analyst would produce.

## What startups do in Sourcetable

- **Growth metrics** — track signups, activation, retention, and referral from your product database
- **Financial models** — build revenue projections and scenario models from live data
- **Investor reports** — generate metrics and narratives for board meetings and fundraising
- **Ad hoc analysis** — answer questions on the fly without building a data pipeline

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Growth dashboard | Connect your database, ask "Show weekly signups, activation rate, and WAU" |
| Financial model | Connect Stripe, ask "Project revenue for the next 12 months at current growth rate" |
| Cohort analysis | Ask "Show monthly retention curves for the last 6 cohorts" |
| Competitive analysis | Ask the AI to research competitor pricing and features |
