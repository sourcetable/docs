Sourcetable helps finance teams build revenue models, track budgets, and close the books faster — all from live data, without manual pulls.

## What finance teams do in Sourcetable

- **Revenue models** — connect Stripe, QuickBooks, or your database and build live revenue models
- **P&L summaries** — ask the AI to generate profit and loss statements from your accounting data
- **Budget vs. actuals** — track spending against budget with auto-updating dashboards
- **Month-end close** — pull data from all sources, reconcile, and generate reports in minutes

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Monthly revenue report | Connect Stripe, ask "Show monthly revenue trends for the past 12 months" |
| Budget tracking | Import budget data, connect actuals, ask "Compare budget vs. actuals by department" |
| Cash flow forecast | Ask "Forecast cash flow for the next 6 months based on historical patterns" |
| Expense analysis | Connect QuickBooks, ask "Break down expenses by category and flag any anomalies" |
