Connect your ad platforms, CRM, and analytics tools to Sourcetable and let the AI analyze campaign performance, customer segments, and attribution.

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|------|-------------|
| Campaign performance | Connect Google Ads and ask "Compare CPA and ROAS across all campaigns this month" |
| Customer segmentation | "Cluster customers into segments based on purchase frequency, recency, and value" |
| Attribution analysis | "Show me which channels drive the most conversions with a multi-touch model" |
| Funnel analysis | "Calculate conversion rates at each stage of the signup funnel" |
| Competitive research | "Research competitor pricing for [product category]" using deep research |
| Report generation | "Create a weekly marketing dashboard with channel performance and trends" |
