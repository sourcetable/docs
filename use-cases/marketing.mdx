Sourcetable connects to your ad platforms, CRM, and analytics tools so you can analyze campaign performance, build attribution models, and generate reports — all in one spreadsheet.

## What marketing teams do in Sourcetable

- **Campaign performance** — pull data from Google Ads, Facebook Ads, TikTok Ads, and more into one view
- **Attribution modeling** — combine ad data with CRM data to see which channels drive revenue
- **Weekly/monthly reports** — auto-generate performance summaries for stakeholders
- **Customer behavior** — analyze engagement, retention, and LTV from your analytics data

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Cross-channel ad performance | Connect Google Ads + Facebook Ads, ask "Compare ROAS across channels" |
| Campaign ROI | Connect ad data + Stripe, ask "Which campaigns drove the most revenue?" |
| Email performance | Connect Mailchimp, ask "Show open rates and click rates by campaign" |
| SEO tracking | Connect Google Search Console, ask "What keywords are driving the most traffic?" |
