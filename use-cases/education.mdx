Sourcetable helps educators grade, analyze, and create teaching materials — with AI assistance that saves hours per week.

## What educators do in Sourcetable

- **Grade assignments** — import grades and ask the AI to calculate weighted averages, curves, and final scores
- **Student performance** — analyze trends, identify at-risk students, and track progress over time
- **Teaching materials** — generate practice problems, datasets for lessons, and rubrics
- **Academic reporting** — create class summaries, grade distributions, and progress reports

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Grade calculation | Import grade data, ask "Calculate final grades with 30% homework, 30% midterm, 40% final" |
| Performance analysis | Ask "Which students are trending downward compared to their first exam?" |
| Generate practice data | Ask "Create a sample dataset of 100 rows for a statistics lesson on regression" |
| Class summary | Ask "Summarize grade distributions by assignment and identify outliers" |
