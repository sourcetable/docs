Sourcetable helps HR and people teams analyze workforce data, plan headcount, and benchmark compensation — without starting from a blank spreadsheet every quarter.

## What HR teams do in Sourcetable

- **Headcount planning** — model hiring scenarios based on growth targets and budget constraints
- **Attrition analysis** — identify turnover patterns and predict flight risk
- **Compensation benchmarking** — analyze pay equity and comp ranges across roles
- **People analytics** — track engagement, performance, and demographic trends

## Example workflows

| Task | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Headcount model | Import headcount data, ask "Model headcount growth at 15%, 20%, and 25% annual rates" |
| Turnover analysis | Ask "What's the attrition rate by department and tenure?" |
| Comp analysis | Upload compensation data, ask "Show pay distribution by role and flag outliers" |
| Diversity metrics | Ask "Break down headcount by department, level, and demographic" |
