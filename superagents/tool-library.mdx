## How tools are selected

When you send a message, the AI:

1. Analyzes your intent
2. Selects the appropriate tool(s) from the library
3. Executes the tool with the right parameters
4. Returns results to your spreadsheet

You can also ask "What tools can you use?" to see available capabilities for your current context.
