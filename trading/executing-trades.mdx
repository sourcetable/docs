Sourcetable integrates with Robinhood to let you buy and sell directly from the AI chat. Go from analysis to execution without switching apps.

<Warning>
  Trading involves risk of loss. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Sourcetable does not provide financial advice.
</Warning>

## Supported assets

- **Stocks** — buy and sell US equities
- **Crypto** — trade cryptocurrencies
- **Fractional shares** — buy partial shares of expensive stocks (e.g., $100 of BRK.A)

## Order types

- **Market orders** — execute at the current market price
- **Limit orders** — set a maximum buy price or minimum sell price

## Placing trades

Ask the AI:

- "Buy 10 shares of AAPL"
- "Sell 50 shares of TSLA at market price"
- "Place a limit order to buy 100 shares of MSFT at $400"
- "Buy $500 worth of Bitcoin"
- "Buy 0.5 shares of AMZN"

## Paper trading

Test strategies without risking real money. Paper trades simulate real market conditions without executing actual transactions.

- "Paper trade: buy 100 shares of NVDA"
- "Run this momentum strategy in paper trading mode"

## Connecting Robinhood

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI chat">
    Press `Cmd+\` (Mac) or `Ctrl+\` (Windows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add credentials">
    Ask the AI to connect Robinhood, or go to connector settings. Enter your Robinhood credentials when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Complete two-factor authentication if required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start trading">
    You can now place trades through the AI chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security

- Robinhood credentials are encrypted and stored securely
- Sourcetable never stores your brokerage password in plain text
- All trade requests are confirmed before execution
- You can disconnect Robinhood at any time from settings
