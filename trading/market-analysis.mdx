Sourcetable provides 25+ financial AI tools for analyzing individual stocks, sectors, and the broader market. Ask the AI in natural language and it runs the appropriate analysis.

## Fundamental analysis

Examine company financials to assess intrinsic value:

- "Run a fundamental analysis on AAPL"
- "What's MSFT's return on equity and profit margin trend?"
- "Build a DCF model for NVDA with a 10% discount rate"
- "Compare P/E ratios of FAANG stocks"

Tools: FundamentalAnalyst, FactorAnalyst, ValueInvestor, GrowthInvestor, ContrarianInvestor, DCFAnalyst

## Technical analysis

Analyze price patterns and indicators:

- "Show RSI and MACD for TSLA over the last 6 months"
- "Is SPY above its 200-day moving average?"
- "Calculate Bollinger Bands for AMZN"
- "Generate a candlestick chart with volume for GOOGL"

Tools: TechnicalAnalyst, FuturesAnalyst

### Available indicators
SMA, EMA, WMA, RSI, MACD, Stochastic Oscillator, Bollinger Bands, ATR, VWAP, OBV, ADX, Parabolic SAR, Ichimoku Cloud

## Sentiment analysis

Gauge market sentiment from news and social media:
