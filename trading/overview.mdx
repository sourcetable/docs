Sourcetable integrates market analysis with trade execution, so you can research, analyze, and trade — all from the same spreadsheet. The financial AI tools provide institutional-grade analysis, and the Robinhood integration lets you execute trades without leaving the app.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Research">
    Use financial AI tools to analyze stocks, sectors, or markets. Ask the AI to run fundamental analysis, technical analysis, or sentiment analysis on any ticker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze">
    Build financial models, run backtests, stress tests, and Monte Carlo simulations. Compare strategies and assess risk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Review the AI's analysis, check portfolio impact, and make your investment decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute">
    Place trades through Robinhood directly from the AI chat. Buy or sell stocks, crypto, and fractional shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor">
    Track positions, review portfolio performance, and set up automated reporting for ongoing monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>
