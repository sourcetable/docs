---
title: Portfolio tracking
description: Monitor positions, track performance, and analyze portfolio risk in Sourcetable.
---

Once you've connected Robinhood, Sourcetable can pull your portfolio data and provide ongoing analysis.

## Viewing your portfolio

Ask the AI:

- "Show me my current positions"
- "What's my portfolio value?"
- "List my holdings with current prices and P&L"
- "Show my trade history for the last month"

## Performance analysis

- "What's my portfolio return this year?"
- "Compare my performance against the S&P 500"
- "Which positions are contributing the most to my returns?"
- "Show my portfolio's Sharpe ratio"

## Risk analysis

Use the risk management tools on your actual portfolio:

- "What's the VaR of my current portfolio?"
- "Stress test my holdings against the 2020 COVID crash"
- "Run a Monte Carlo simulation for my portfolio over the next year"
- "Show the correlation matrix of my holdings"

See [risk management](/quant/risk-management) for more details on available risk tools.

## Portfolio optimization

Ask the AI to suggest allocation improvements:

- "How should I rebalance to maximize the Sharpe ratio?"
- "What should I add to improve diversification?"
- "Am I overexposed to any sector?"

## Automated monitoring

Set up automated reports to track your portfolio over time:

- "Create a daily summary of my portfolio positions and P&L"
- "Set up a weekly report comparing my performance to benchmarks"

See [automated reporting](/ai/automated-reporting) for details on recurring reports.
