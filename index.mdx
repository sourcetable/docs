Sourcetable is an AI-powered spreadsheet that combines the familiarity of Excel with the intelligence of AI and the power of Python. It connects directly to your databases and SaaS tools, so you can analyze, transform, and act on your data without leaving your spreadsheet.

## Why Sourcetable

- **Full spreadsheet** — XLSX, CSV, and PDF compatible with formula parity to Excel and Google Sheets
- **Native AI assistant** — ask questions in plain English, get answers as formulas, charts, tables, or written explanations
- **80+ data connectors** — connect to PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, Stripe, Salesforce, HubSpot, and more
- **Python under the hood** — access pandas, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, matplotlib, and plotly without writing code
- **Hallucination-free** — answers are grounded in your actual data, not fabricated
