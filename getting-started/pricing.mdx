## AI models

All plans use Sourcetable's default AI model. Pro and Max plans also provide access to:

- GPT-4
- Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- Llama 3.3 70B on Groq

## Education discount

Students and faculty get **50% off** Pro and Max plans. Contact [team@sourcetable.com](mailto:team@sourcetable.com) with your .edu email to apply.

## Connectors

Data connectors are available on Pro (up to 3) and Max/Enterprise (unlimited). Connectors include 25+ database types and 60+ SaaS integrations. See [Connectors](/connectors/overview) for the full list.
