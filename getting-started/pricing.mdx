Sourcetable offers four plans. All plans include the core AI spreadsheet with formulas, charts, pivot tables, and collaboration.

## Plans

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free | $39/user/mo | $100/user/mo | Custom |

| **AI models** | Default model | All models (GPT-4, Claude, Llama) | All models | All models + custom |

<Note>
\* "Unlimited" on the Max plan is subject to fair use guardrails.
</Note>

## What every plan includes

- AI spreadsheet assistant with analysis, insights, and summarization
- Magic autofill, data generation, and AI research
- Smart formulas, pivot tables, charts, and visualizations
- Collaboration and sharing
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Import/export (XLSX, CSV, JSON, PDF, Parquet, and more)

## AI credits

Some AI operations consume credits. You can purchase additional AI credits from **Settings > Billing** if you need more on the Pro plan.

## Education discount

Students and faculty get **50% off** Pro and Max plans. Contact [team@sourcetable.com](mailto:team@sourcetable.com) with your .edu email to apply.

## Enterprise

For teams of 15+ seats, Enterprise plans include:

- Advanced security features
- SSO (Single Sign-On)
- Dedicated support and white-glove onboarding
- Custom AI model configurations
- Cloud or on-premises deployment options

Contact [team@sourcetable.com](mailto:team@sourcetable.com) for Enterprise pricing.
