## Enterprise

For teams of 15+ seats that need advanced security, SSO, dedicated support, and white-glove onboarding, see the [Enterprise page](https://sourcetable.com/enterprise) or contact [team@sourcetable.com](mailto:team@sourcetable.com).

## AI models

Sourcetable is powered by the latest AI models from OpenAI, Claude, Llama, and Hugging Face. Pro and Max plans unlock premium models and expanded capabilities.

## Education discount

Students and faculty get **50% off** Pro and Max plans. Sign up with your .edu email and select the Education plan on the billing page, or contact [education@sourcetable.com](mailto:education@sourcetable.com).

## Data connectors

Data connectors let you query databases and applications using AI. Available on Pro (up to 3) and Max (unlimited). Featured connectors include Supabase, BigQuery, Google Analytics 4, PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Google Ads, MySQL, and DuckDB. See [Connectors](/connectors/overview) for the full list.
